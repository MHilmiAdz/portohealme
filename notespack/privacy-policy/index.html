Privacy Policy

Application: NotesPack
Effective Date: August 2026
Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to NotesPack ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and stores your information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Account Information (Authentication)

When you sign in using Google Sign-In (powered by Firebase Authentication), we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture URL
  • A unique Firebase user identification string (UID)

This information is used strictly to identify your account and secure your session.

B. User Notes and Content

Any notes, checklists, text entries, and expense amounts you create within NotesPack are stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database (Android Room). We do not view, rent, or sell your private note contents.

C. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to scan text documents or receipts for optical character recognition. Images are processed on-device.
  • Internet: Required to authenticate your account via Firebase.

2. On-Device Text Recognition (OCR)

NotesPack uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to allow you to extract text from physical receipts. Text extraction is performed locally on-device.

3. Third-Party Services

NotesPack uses trusted third-party services for authentication:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Google ML Kit

4. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize the security of your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: Communications with Firebase authentication servers use modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Local Sandboxing: Your local database is stored in your device's protected application sandbox.

5. Account Deletion

How to Delete Your Account and Data:
  • Email Request: You can request immediate deletion of your authentication credentials and associated data by emailing us at projectshealme@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days.
  • Local Data: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local notes and preferences stored on your device.
