Core Capabilities

Powerfully Simple. Completely Private.

Essential productivity and financial tracking tools united into a single, cohesive interface.

📝

Rich Text & Chapters

Capture structured ideas with headings, bullet points, checklists, and auto-organized multi-chapter notes.

📷

On-Device Receipt OCR

Instantly extract itemized lines and prices from paper receipts using offline Google ML Kit text recognition.

👥

Smart Bill Splitter

Calculate fair group shares with flexible itemized or equal splitting, custom tax ratios, and tip handling.

📊

Monthly Spending Recap

Understand your cashflow at a glance with monthly expenditure breakdowns and category tracking.

🎨

Freehand Canvas Drawing

Sketch diagrams, brainstorm visual concepts, or jot handwritten thoughts with multi-color stroke tools.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

Zero tracking, zero cloud telemetry. All data is stored locally inside your private Android Room SQLite database.
