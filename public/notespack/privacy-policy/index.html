2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Authentication Data

Google Sign-In is required to initialize the application. When you sign in using Google Sign-In (powered by Firebase Authentication and Android Credential Manager), we receive and store:

  • Your full name (as provided by your Google profile)
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture URL
  • A unique Firebase User Identifier (UID)

This authentication information is used strictly to establish and verify your session identity. All note content, drawings, receipts, and user data remain stored locally on your device.

B. User Notes & Financial Content (Stored Locally)

Any notes, spending entries, split bills, checklists, drawings, itineraries, recipes, item/due-date reminders, and subscription schedules you create within NotesPack are stored locally on your device in an on-device SQLite database (Room).

Locally captured or attached media—such as physical receipt scans and recipe dish photos (stored in local application storage under dish_photos/)—remain strictly on your device. They are never uploaded to remote servers or shared with third parties. When you perform an export, these images are packaged directly inside your user-initiated, encrypted .notespack archive.

NotesPack features a secure Vault. Notes stored inside the Vault, as well as sensitive due-date verification PINs, are encrypted at the field level on-device using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption via the Android KeyStore. We do not have access to your private encryption keys or unencrypted vault content.

C. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used exclusively when you choose to capture physical receipts for optical character recognition (OCR), capture recipe or dish photos, or scan offline QR codes for due date reminders. Images are processed and stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications: Used to dispatch timely reminder alerts for item/due-date reminders and recurring subscription renewals scheduled by you. Reminder notifications strictly adhere to system ringer modes, silent and vibrate settings, and alarm audio stream volume controls.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used via Android's AlarmManager to guarantee that due date alerts and subscription billing reminders fire precisely at the user-scheduled minute.
  • Biometrics: Used to unlock the on-device Vault. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by the Android operating system's BiometricPrompt framework; NotesPack never receives, processes, or stores raw biometric or fingerprint data.

3. Network Connections & Third-Party Services

While NotesPack operates primarily on-device, internet access is utilized for specific explicit functionalities:

  • Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication: Communicates with Google identity and Firebase servers to authenticate your Google profile.
  • Link Previews: When you save a web link in a Link Collection note, NotesPack connects directly via HTTPS to the destination website to retrieve Open Graph preview metadata (title, description, and image URL) and loads the site's favicon. Connecting directly to that website necessarily reveals your device's IP address and standard HTTP request headers to the third-party website hosting that page.
  • Google Play In-App Review: Utilizes the official Google Play In-App Review API to let users rate the app.
  • Google Play Services & ML Kit: On-device text recognition, Document Scanning, and Barcode Scanning utilize ML Kit models provided by Google Play services. Google Play services may transmit operational telemetry and diagnostic metrics to Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, our processing of your personal data is grounded in the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Processing authentication data to maintain your account and deliver app features you explicitly invoke.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Generating rich link previews upon your instruction, securing the app against unauthorized access, and debugging crash reports.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where requested by your device operating system for optional runtime permissions (Camera, Notifications). You may revoke consent at any time via Android System Settings.

5. International Data Transfers

Firebase Authentication and Google Play services are hosted by Google LLC on servers located in the United States and globally. Data transfers are governed by European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Google's Data Processing and Security Terms to ensure adequate protection.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Authentication credentials remain stored with Firebase Authentication for as long as your account remains active. Local note databases and media files remain on your device until deleted by you or until the app is uninstalled.

You can delete your account at any time through either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open NotesPack, navigate to Profile → Settings & Backup → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication profile and signs you out.
  2. Email Request: Email projectshealme@gmail.com from the email address associated with your NotesPack account requesting account deletion. Requests are processed within thirty (30) days.

7. Backup Files (.notespack)

NotesPack allows you to export your data and local media (including physical receipt scans and recipe dish photos) into a portable archive file (.notespack). You may optionally protect this backup with a custom password, which encrypts the entire archive using PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 (210,000 iterations) and AES-256-GCM. The backup archive is generated entirely on your device and saved to a location of your choice via the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF). Once exported or shared, the security and confidentiality of the backup file is the sole responsibility of the user.

8. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area under GDPR, the United Kingdom under UK GDPR, Indonesia under UU PDP No. 27/2022, and Brazil under LGPD), you possess statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request confirmation and copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain and export your notes and account data via our backup utility.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to specific data processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw permission for notifications or camera at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at projectshealme@gmail.com.
